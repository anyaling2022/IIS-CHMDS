# IIS-CHMDS
Intelligent Identification System of Chinese Herbal Medicine Decoction Slices


The Intelligent Identification System for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) Decoction Pieces has been developed and is maintained by the National Engineering Laboratory for TCM Standardization Technology. This system encompasses an extensive database comprising 235 commonly used decoction pieces, all of which are included in the Chinese Pharmacopoeia, spanning across 55,996 batches of data. It supports the extraction and processing of data from unknown samples as well as the prediction of their varieties. For each prediction result, if the similarity exceeds 0.89, the predicted variety is deemed correct; if the similarity is less than 0.89, the unknown sample is considered to belong to other varieties beyond the 235 known ones.
